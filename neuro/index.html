OpenClaw Neuro

OpenClaw Neuro is a private Google Workspace integration operated by Anatoliy Martynov on a dedicated Windows computer. It helps its owner work with Google Drive files, Google Sheets and Google Docs through the owner's OpenClaw assistant and Telegram Neuro topic. It is not offered as a public service.

Google access

The integration requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive. This scope permits broad access to the connected account's Drive files. The application uses it to search and read files and to create, edit, copy, move and move approved files to Trash, including Google Sheets and Google Docs.

Application controls limit writes to registered Neuro resources and newly created files in its operational area. Existing business sources are not automatically approved for writing. Changes to sharing permissions, ownership transfers and permanent deletion are unavailable through this integration.

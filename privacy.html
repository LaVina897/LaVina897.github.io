Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

LaVina AI-PC Encrypted Backup is used only by its owner. It requests Google Drive drive.file access to create, list, read and update the backup files it uses. It does not request access to unrelated Drive files.

Backup contents are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload to the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are stored in an encrypted local configuration. The recovery passphrase is held separately by the owner. Operational records contain backup status and timestamps, not passwords, tokens or unencrypted backup contents.

No Google user data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for model training. The owner can revoke this application's access in Google Account settings and remove its backup files from Drive.
